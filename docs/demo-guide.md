# Record a clear VibeLeak Atlas demonstration

Use the real native plugin and your own authorized website. Do not present QA fixture reports as live security results. Hide private keys, account details, repository paths and sensitive evidence before recording.

## A short story

1. Open Atlas on the dithered globe. Say: "Assess a website you own, understand the evidence, and work on a reviewed fix in Hermes."
2. Switch to ASCII, then back to Globe. Explain that map markers are your approved assessments at approximate hosting-country positions, not worldwide attacks or verified server origins.
3. Enter your authorized hostname and choose **Review assessment**. Show the exact target and one-unit ceiling. The two approvals make the permission and cost clear.
4. Run only the assessment you deliberately authorized. Select its local record and show one real finding, its source evidence and proposed investigation.
5. Optionally choose **Locate my approved sites** and approve the DNS disclosure. Select a country or use List to inspect the website. Unknown hosting stays unlocated.
6. Open **Prepare fix-plan draft**. Show that it asks Hermes to confirm the repository and plan the smallest justified change. Appending creates an unsent draft; it does not silently modify code.
7. After a real approved fix and deployment, record **Review rescan** and the report comparison. Explain what persisted, what appeared and what was not observed again. Do not call an issue fixed solely because a grade improved.

## What to say

"The map is a way to navigate your own assessments. The useful part is the evidence and the next approved action. Atlas keeps the assessment, fix-plan handoff and rescan together without importing other people's website reports."

For PHP: "The advisory view distinguishes a header observation from owner runtime evidence and vendor backports. It does not prove exploitation or patch PHP for you."

## Recording checks

- Use a current matching workbench backend and renderer, a configured scoped owner key, and a website you own or have explicit permission to assess.
- Get permission to publish the target name and findings. Use a clean demonstration repository instead of a client workspace where possible.
- Record the actual globe/ASCII transitions, confirmation dialog, finding preview and comparison. No imitation of Hermes UI or invented results.
- Keep the QA fixture banner visible in any internal QA clip. Such clips are not evidence of a live scan and should not be marketed as one.
- Avoid showing a secret editor, account billing information, personal chat history or a clipboard containing private findings.
- No release, upload or public post is created by this guide.
